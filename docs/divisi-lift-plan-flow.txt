KONTEKS TRAKINDO
=====================================================================
Trakindo adalah dealer Caterpillar di Indonesia. Mereka punya
4 divisi utama yang masing-masing punya alasan berbeda kenapa
perlu lift plan.

=====================================================================
1. DIVISI RENTAL — Paling Sering
=====================================================================

CASE:
  Customer sewa crane Cat 336 (100 ton) untuk proyek konstruksi
  di Balikpapan. Crane dikirim ke site customer.

FLOW:
  Customer request rental crane
    -> Trakindo Rental divisi proses
    -> Crane dikirim ke site + operator
    -> SEBELUM lifting, operator WAJIB buat lift plan
    -> Lift plan disetujui supervisor -> lifting boleh dimulai

KENAPA LIFT PLAN PENTING?
  Kalau crane jatuh/kelebihan beban di site customer ->
  Trakindo yang tanggung jawab (karena crane milik Trakindo +
  operator Trakindo). Lift plan = bukti bahwa Trakindo sudah
  lakukan due diligence.

CONTOH CASE NYATA:
  - Customer mau angkat boiler 40 ton di refinery
  - Operator Trakindo buat lift plan: crane Cat 336, radius 12m,
    sling 4-leg 60 derajat
  - Supervisor approve -> lifting aman -> tidak ada insiden

=====================================================================
2. DIVISI SERVICE — Internal Lifting
=====================================================================

CASE:
  Tim service Trakindo harus bongkar engine dari unit customer
  (excavator Cat 320) di workshop Trakindo.

FLOW:
  Customer kirim unit ke workshop Trakindo
    -> Service divisi diagnosa: engine harus keluar
    -> Engine weighs 3.5 ton -> perlu crane di workshop
    -> Mechanic buat lift plan untuk bongkar engine
    -> Supervisor service approve -> bongkar aman

KENAPA LIFT PLAN PENTING?
  Di workshop Trakindo, kalau engine jatuh saat dibongkar ->
  cidera karyawan + kerusakan engine (engine Cat mahal).
  Lift plan = SOP keselamatan internal.

CONTOH CASE NYATA:
  - Bongkar counterweight excavator Cat 385 (8 ton) di workshop
  - Bongkar boom assembly crane (12 ton) untuk repair
  - Angkat hydraulic cylinder (2 ton) untuk replacement

=====================================================================
3. DIVISI PARTS — Warehouse Lifting
=====================================================================

CASE:
  Gudang parts Trakindo terima container berisi spare parts besar
  (transmission assembly, engine assembly).

FLOW:
  Container tiba di gudang Trakindo
    -> Parts divisi perlu unload dari container
    -> Transmission assembly = 1.8 ton -> perlu forklift/crane kecil
    -> Warehouse staff buat lift plan sederhana
    -> Supervisor approve -> unloading aman

KENAPA LIFT PLAN PENTING?
  Kalau transmission jatuh saat unloading ->
  kerusakan parts + cidera warehouse staff. Parts Cat sangat mahal.

=====================================================================
4. DIVISI SALES — Demo & Delivery
=====================================================================

CASE:
  Customer beli crane baru Cat 345 (250 ton).
  Trakindo kirim ke site customer.

FLOW:
  Customer beli unit via Sales divisi
    -> Unit dikirim ke site customer
    -> Commissioning: crane harus di-setup, test lift
    -> Sales/field service buat lift plan untuk test lift
    -> Supervisor approve -> test lift aman -> handover ke customer

KENAPA LIFT PLAN PENTING?
  Saat commissioning/demo, crane masih dalam tanggung jawab
  Trakindo. Kalau ada insiden saat test lift ->
  Trakindo yang jawab.

=====================================================================
KENAPA DIVISI DIPILIH DI STEP 1?
=====================================================================

Step 1: Pilih Divisi
  |
  v
Menentukan:
  1. SIAPA yang buat lift plan
     - Operator rental
     - Mechanic service
     - Warehouse staff parts
     - Field service sales

  2. SIAPA yang approve
     - Supervisor divisi tersebut

  3. LOKASI lifting
     - Site customer (rental)
     - Workshop Trakindo (service)
     - Gudang Trakindo (parts)
     - Commissioning site (sales)

  4. JENIS beban yang umum
     - Rental -> beragam (tergantung request customer)
     - Service -> component (engine, transmission, counterweight)
     - Parts -> spare parts (transmission assembly, engine assembly)
     - Sales -> unit assembly (commissioning, test lift)
